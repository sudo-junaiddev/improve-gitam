1. Apple iPhone (iOS)

iOS 18, 17, 16, 15, 14

  1. Go to Settings > Wi-Fi.
  2. Tap on GITAM or GITAM-5GHz.
  3. Disable Fixed / Private MAC Address:
    • On iOS 18+: Tap Private Wi-Fi Address and select Fixed or turn off.
    • On iOS 14 - 17: Turn off Private Wi-Fi Address.
  4. Turn off Limit IP Address Tracking and iCloud Private Relay for this network.
  5. Enter password Gitam$$123 and click Join.
  6. Tap info (i) icon on GITAM-5GHz, find Wi-Fi Address, and hold to copy MAC.
  7. Go to macenrollment.gitam.edu and log in.
  8. If you have a laptop, enter your laptop MAC address there, else enter your phone MAC in place of the laptop MAC.
  9. Click Submit.

2. Samsung (One UI)

Android 10 - 15 (One UI 2.0 - 7.x)

  1. Go to Settings > Connections > Wi-Fi.
  2. Select GITAM or GITAM-5GHz.
  3. WAIT! Do not connect yet. Tap View more or Advanced.
  4. Change MAC address type to Phone MAC (or turn off Randomize MAC).
  5. Enter password Gitam$$123 and tap Connect.
  6. Go to Settings > About Phone > Status Information.
  7. Scroll down to Wi-Fi MAC Address and press-and-hold to copy.
  8. Log in to macenrollment.gitam.edu and submit your MAC.

Android 9 (One UI 1.x)

  1. Go to Settings > Connections > Wi-Fi.
  2. Select GITAM-5GHz > Advanced.
  3. Under Privacy, change to Phone MAC.
  4. Connect using Gitam$$123 and copy MAC from About Phone > Status.

3. Google Pixel & Stock Android (Motorola)

Android 12 - 15

  1. Go to Settings > Network & Internet > Internet.
  2. Select GITAM or GITAM-5GHz.
  3. Tap Privacy and set to Use Device MAC.
  4. Enter password Gitam$$123 and connect.
  5. Copy MAC from Settings > About Phone > Wi-Fi MAC Address.

Android 9, 10, 11

  1. Go to Settings > Network & Internet > Wi-Fi.
  2. Select GITAM-5GHz > Advanced > Privacy.
  3. Change to Use Device MAC and connect using Gitam$$123.

4. Nothing Phone (Nothing OS)

Nothing OS 1.0 - 3.x (Android 12 - 15)

  1. Go to Settings > Network & Internet > Internet.
  2. Select GITAM or GITAM-5GHz > Privacy.
  3. Change Use randomized MAC to Use device MAC.
  4. Enter password Gitam$$123 and connect.
  5. Copy MAC from Settings > About Phone > Device Details.

5. Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO (MIUI / HyperOS)

HyperOS & MIUI 12 / 13 / 14 (Android 11 - 15)

  1. Go to Settings > Wi-Fi.
  2. Click on arrow/icon next to GITAM or GITAM-5GHz.
  3. Tap Privacy and select Use Device MAC.
  4. Enter password Gitam$$123 and connect.
  5. Copy MAC from About Phone > Detailed Specs > Status.

MIUI 10 / 11 (Android 9 - 10)

  1. Go to Wi-Fi > GITAM-5GHz > Advanced Settings.
  2. Set Privacy to Use Device MAC and connect with Gitam$$123.

6. OnePlus / OPPO / Realme

Android 11 - 15 (OxygenOS / ColorOS / realme UI)

  1. Go to Settings > Wi-Fi.
  2. Select GITAM or GITAM-5GHz.
  3. Tap Privacy or Wi-Fi MAC Address Type > Use Device MAC.
  4. Enter password Gitam$$123 and connect.
  5. Copy MAC from Settings > About Device > Status.

Android 9 & 10

  1. Go to Wi-Fi > GITAM-5GHz > Advanced.
  2. Set Privacy to Use Device MAC and connect with Gitam$$123.

7. Vivo / iQOO (Funtouch OS / OriginOS)

8. Desktop Platforms (Windows, Mac OS, Linux)

Windows

  1. Press WIN + R, type cmd.exe.
  2. Run command:
getmac /v

Look for Wireless / WAN adapter MAC address.

Mac OS

  1. Open Terminal.
  2. Run command:
networksetup -listallhardwareports | grep "WIFI"

Copy the displayed Wi-Fi MAC address.

Linux

  1. Open Terminal.
  2. Run command:
ip route get 1 | awk '/dev/ {print $5}' | xargs -I{} cat /sys/class/net/{}/address

Works for Ubuntu, Fedora, Debian & Dual-Boot setups.

MAC Enrollment Instructions

  1. Go to macenrollment.gitam.edu and log in with your credentials.
  2. If you have a laptop, add your laptop MAC address. If not, enter your phone MAC address in place of the laptop MAC address.
  3. Click Submit — Done!
